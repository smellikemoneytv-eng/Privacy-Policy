# Privacy-Policy
CardCrew's Privacy Policy
CardCrew is a Windows application operated by Smellikemoney LLC. It helps its operator research trading cards, review eBay listings and sales, manage purchase and inventory records, and prepare fulfillment. This policy describes the information processed by the CardCrew application and its separately hosted eBay account-deletion notification endpoint.

Information processed by CardCrew
Card research and records: card names and printing details, listing links and prices, comparable-sale evidence, watchlist profiles, inventory, purchase and resale records, and activity entered or collected by the operator.
Sold-history searches: if configured, CardCrew sends the searched card’s identifying details and marketplace search parameters to The Card API to find completed-sale records. Returned titles, prices, dates, grades, and links are stored in CardCrew’s local database.
eBay connection and orders: the eBay application credentials and authorization tokens supplied or issued when eBay is connected; public listing details; and checkout, shipping, and order information needed for eBay operations. CardCrew does not ask for an eBay password. Payment-card details are entered with eBay and are not received or stored by CardCrew.
Addresses and shipping: the operator’s receiving/ship-from address and, when needed to fulfill an order, a buyer’s delivery details. CardCrew sends relevant addresses to eBay, Shippo, or the selected carrier when the operator requests checkout or shipping services.
eBay deletion notifications: eBay may send account-deletion notification requests to CardCrew’s callback endpoint. The endpoint is designed to acknowledge these requests without keeping eBay member identifiers.
How information is used
CardCrew uses information to find and evaluate listings for the identified card printing, retrieve and display completed-sale evidence, apply the operator’s buying rules, submit an eBay checkout when live buying has been enabled, maintain local inventory and accounting records, prepare shipping, and handle eBay deletion notifications. CardCrew does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing
The Windows application keeps its operational database on the operator’s computer. Where available, Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) encrypts eBay credentials and tokens for the current Windows user. Buyer-address fields are protected using Windows DPAPI where supported. Information is sent to eBay when CardCrew calls eBay services, to The Card API for configured sold-history searches, and to Shippo or a carrier when the operator requests shipping services. Those providers process information under their own policies and terms. CardCrew’s Railway-hosted eBay callback is separate from the local card database.

Retention and deletion
Local CardCrew records remain on the operator’s computer until the operator deletes them or removes CardCrew’s application data. CardCrew does not currently enforce an automatic retention schedule for local records. Third-party providers may retain information under their own policies and legal obligations. To request help with information CardCrew controls, use the contact below.

Security
CardCrew uses local protections appropriate to its design, including DPAPI encryption for supported credentials and address fields. No security measure can guarantee complete protection. The operator is responsible for protecting the computer and its Windows account. Encrypted credentials are tied to the Windows user who saved them and are not portable working credentials.

Children and changes
CardCrew is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. This policy may be updated when the software or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
